Our Purpose & Mission

Why English Booster?

English Booster connects people from different cultures and countries through one universal language: English.

✨ Core Mission Authentic Global Connection

Our platform connects learners worldwide to practice English in real time through authentic conversations, via messaging and live calls. Designed to break down language barriers, it fosters supportive cultural exchange and mutual learning between partners with shared interests. We provide an interactive, inclusive space where daily practice turns language fluency into genuine human connections.

🤝

Human Connection First

Fluency does not come from memorizing vocabulary lists in isolation. It emerges when you interact with real people who listen, respond, and share genuine life stories.

🤖

Intelligent Pedagogical AI

Our AI acts as an encouraging mentor in the background — offering gentle grammatical corrections with explanations so you learn naturally without fear.

🌍

Global Inclusivity

From Abidjan and Tokyo to Madrid and Lagos, we welcome learners of all cultural backgrounds and CEFR proficiency levels from A1 beginners to C2 proficients.

FAQ

Common Questions

What if I make mistakes while speaking? +
Making mistakes is the foundation of spoken language acquisition! English Booster fosters a supportive, zero-judgment atmosphere. Both your partner and our AI assistant are here to help you grow.
How does the AI Match Score work? +
Our algorithm evaluates your CEFR level, target goals (e.g. travel vs. career), shared interests, and typical availability timezones to suggest the most compatible practice partners.
Can I practice using audio without turning on my video? +
Yes! You have full control. You can exchange voice notes in chat, join audio-only calls, or turn on your camera whenever you feel comfortable.
How can I reach the founder or report feedback? +
You can reach Tychique Bongo directly via WhatsApp (+242 905 37 12), phone (+225 07 05 88 46 87), or email. We read and implement community feedback every single week!

Legal & Community Guidelines

English Booster is dedicated to polite, respectful international exchange. Harassment, discriminatory language, and commercial spam are strictly prohibited. Conversations are protected with industry-standard TLS encryption.
