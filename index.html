✨ AI Grammar Correction Preview Live Example
❌
Learner message
I have went to London yesterday.
✓
Natural English correction
I went to London yesterday.
Explanation: Use "went" because "yesterday" refers to a finished, completed time in the past. The present perfect "have gone" is used for unstated or ongoing timeframes.
